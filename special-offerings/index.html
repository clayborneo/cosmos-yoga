The Cosmos Weekend Deal

Our one sale of the year, for the three days we celebrate shopping small. Friday through Sunday only.

Claim the deal

Three days, one deal

From Black Friday through Sunday, the deal: for example, $X off an annual membership, a free month added to a 10-class pack, or a gift card bonus. It is the only time of year we do this, and it is as good for a gift as it is for yourself.

Small Business Saturday is our favorite day of the three. Cosmos is a small studio on the Southside, owned and run by the people you see at the front desk, and every membership, pack and gift card bought this weekend keeps the lights (and the candles) on.

How to claim it

  • Online: buy from the Pricing page with code COSMOSWEEKEND, or a timed sale price that appears automatically.
  • At the studio: tell us at the desk before or after any class that weekend.
  • Gift cards: available at the desk and online.

Runs Friday, November 27 through Sunday, November 29, 2026. One per person; cannot be combined with other offers; new memberships start on the purchase date.

Dec 8 – 21

A December ritual

13 Magical Nights

Thirteen candlelit evenings to close out the year: slow practice, sound, stillness and a little celebration, one night at a time.

See the nights

What it is

For two weeks in December the studio turns down the lights. Each of the thirteen nights brings one special evening offering, for example candlelight Yin, a sound bath, a winter solstice circle, restorative with warm compresses, a breathwork night, a movie night, on top of the regular schedule.

Come to one, come to all thirteen. Members: every night is included. Everyone else: a drop-in or class pack covers it, or buy the 13 Nights pass for $X.

The nights

The lineup, one line per night, once the teachers have claimed theirs: Mon Dec 8 · Tue Dec 9 · … · Sun Dec 21 (winter solstice)

How to sign up

  • Each night is a class on the schedule. Reserve your spot in the Mindbody app or from our Classes page, same as any class.
  • Collect a stamp each night you come; 7 of 13 earns a small gift.

December 8 through 21, 2026. Evenings; times vary by night. Spots are limited by the room, so reserve ahead.

Jan 1 – Mar 31

A three-month beginning

The Season of Showing Up

A gentle, three-month season for anyone starting or restarting a practice. No perfect way to begin, no pace to keep, nothing to prove.

Join the season

Welcome to your season

For three months, this space is yours to explore. There is no perfect way to begin, no pace you need to keep, and nothing you need to prove. Just come as you are.

Every student who joins the season gets a welcome kit with an intention card and a Cosmos Passport, gentle invitations to reflect, try something new, and connect with others. Each month brings a reflection email and an optional community gathering.

Three months, three themes

  • JanuaryBegin Where You Are
  • FebruaryMake Space for Connection
  • MarchGrow Into Your Practice

How to join

Who it is for and what it costs: for example, every new member who joins between Jan 1 and Jan 31, or anyone on an intro month, a membership or a 10-pack, no extra charge. Sign up from the Pricing page or at the desk, and your welcome kit is waiting at your first class.

January 1 through March 31, 2027. Gatherings: one Sunday afternoon each month, dates to come.
