The People

Meet the Team

Seasoned facilitators building a home for practice in Chattanooga.

CT

Clay Thomas

Founder

Clay is the founder of Cosmos Yoga. He set out to build an intentionally curated space in Chattanooga where nature, ritual, and movement come together — a studio designed around ease, connection, and community.

SF

Sarah Flanigan

Teacher & Studio Design

Sarah teaches at Cosmos and shapes the feel of the space itself — its design, decor, and the small rituals that make each class an experience. Her classes invite students to slow down, breathe, and reconnect with the body.

LO

Leah Ortiz

Teacher & Studio Operations

Leah teaches at Cosmos and keeps the studio running — coordinating the weekly schedule and the teaching team so every class feels seamless. Her practice blends strength and steadiness with a welcoming, grounded presence.
