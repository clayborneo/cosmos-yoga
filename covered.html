Type a film or series. We look up who made and distributed it and check them against the rights-holder list MPLC sent Cosmos.

Blanket License ground rules (MPLC’s email, Oct 7, 2026, and the agreement’s §4):
  • No admission or other fee for a screening. Concessions and voluntary donations are fine.
  • In public (website, Instagram, posters outside, press): no title, character names or studio name. Genre and your own art only. You may answer with the title if someone calls, texts or emails.
  • Inside the studio and by direct email to members: the title is fine.
  • Any legal copy works: your own disc, a purchase, a rental, a streaming account. Not for outdoor screenings.

How it works: the title search uses Wikidata for the film’s production companies and distributors, then matches those names against the 1,652 rights holders on the list MPLC sent Cosmos (scope “Clubs and Leisure Centers”). It is a strong hint, not a guarantee: a studio can be on the list while a particular title is excluded, and Wikidata can be incomplete. Confirm anything you plan to announce with info@mplc.com.
