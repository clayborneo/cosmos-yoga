Find your practice

Three complementary styles, all levels welcome.

All Levels

Hatha Yoga

An all-levels class focused on alignment, form, and breath. Take your time moving with slow precision and deepening your mind + body connection, as you learn to connect breath with movement.

Strength & Flow

Vinyasa Yoga

Find fluidity in movement as you move to the rhythm of your breath. An opportunity to build strength, challenge your cardiovascular system, and learn to move with your body through intentional + creative sequencing.

Restore

Yin Yoga

Open space for healing + restoration of the mind + body. Experience long holds, supportive props, and guided experiences like meditation + breathwork — the perfect complement to any physical practice.
