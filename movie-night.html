How it works

A movie, a snack bar and forty friends

It's free

Come as you are

Movie night is free. If you'd like to chip in, a $10 donation at the door keeps the popcorn popping and the series going.

The title stays a secret until you walk in. Every film is rated PG-13 or gentler unless we say otherwise.

Bring your seat

Picnic-style seating

Blankets, cushions, floor chairs, low camp chairs: bring what makes you comfortable. We have a stack of yoga mats and bolsters to share.

Space is limited to 40, so save your seat and we'll know to expect you.

Snack bar & the game

Guess the movie

Popcorn, candy and drinks at the door. Read the clues, then DM your guess to @cosmosyogachattanooga. The first right answer wins a free combo.

We only confirm guesses in DMs, so no spoilers in the comments, please.
