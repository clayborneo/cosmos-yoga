i.

13 Magical Nights

A ritual of wishes, reflection & release

ii.

The Practice

Imagine giving yourself permission to dream without needing to know how everything will unfold.

The 13 wishes ritual is an invitation to reflect on what matters and what you hope to welcome into the next season.

iii.

Thirteen Wishes

  1. Begin by writing 13 wishes on separate pieces of paper.
  2. Fold them so you cannot see what you've written.
  3. Each night, release one wish without reading it.

iv.

Release & Trust

The act of releasing each wish symbolizes letting go of the need to control how it comes to life.

The practice invites trust, patience and openness.

v.

The Final Wish

This final intention becomes something you can actively nurture through your own choices and actions.
